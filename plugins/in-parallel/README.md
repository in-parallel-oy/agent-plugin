# In Parallel

Bring your team's context into conversations with your AI assistant. In
Parallel holds your company's workspaces: meetings, decisions, action items,
risks, open questions and wiki pages. This plugin lets your assistant read that
context and record findings you want to share with your team.

## What the plugin contains

- The `in-parallel` skill, which tells your assistant when and how to use In
  Parallel.
- A connection to the In Parallel MCP server at `https://www.in-parallel.ai/mcp`.

It runs no hooks or scripts on your machine, installs no dependencies and keeps
no local files.

## What is sent to In Parallel

Only what your assistant sends through the In Parallel connection. The skill
asks your assistant to:

- read team context, such as briefings, meetings, decisions and wiki pages
- record findings you want to share with your team

## Sign-in

You sign in through your assistant app's connection sign-in, which opens In
Parallel in your browser. The app keeps the sign-in. The plugin never sees,
reads or stores a password or token.

## Try it

- "What needs my attention this week?"
- "Catch me up on my project."
- "Record this finding for my team."

## Help

Ask your assistant: "Check my In Parallel connection." If you can't install the
plugin or access your workspace, ask your workspace administrator, or see
https://support.in-parallel.com/en/.

Setup for other assistants, privacy details and the source are at
https://github.com/in-parallel-oy/agent-plugin.
